ConVía

Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon · 2026

Know it's your ride before you get in.

ConVía is verified carpooling for the university community of Chía, Colombia. Drivers and passengers are students and staff with an institutional e-mail, and the app checks the arriving car with Meta's SAM 3.1 and Muse Spark.

The problem

Congestion in Chía

Every day thousands of students drive alone to the campuses around Chía, on roads that were not built for it. Many cars carry one person while others wait for a bus.

Distrust of strangers

People don't share rides with strangers. ConVía only lets in verified members of the same institution, checks faces on the phone, and lets the passenger confirm the car before getting in.

Built during the hackathon

SAM 3.1 + Muse Spark

Pickup check

The passenger films the arriving car for 3 seconds. SAM 3.1 tracks the car and its plate across the frames, Muse Spark reads plate, brand and color from the best frame, and code decides: it's your ride, don't get in, or film again. About 6–8 s.

Muse Spark

Vehicle autofill

From the vehicle photo, Muse Spark proposes brand, line, color and, only if fully legible, the plate. The driver confirms or corrects every field; the photo is not stored.

Muse Spark

Spanish and English

The English interface is generated from the Spanish texts by a script that sends them to Muse Spark in batches and checks every key and placeholder, then reviewed by hand.

Try it

  1. Open the web app (or install the Android APK) and log in with the demo passenger.
  2. Go to Trips → My requests: the trip with Daniela is accepted.
  3. Tap "Verificar mi carro" and pick a sample video: the trip's car, another car, or the plate covered. The check runs on the real SAM 3.1 + Muse Spark pipeline.
  4. Log in as the demo driver and add a vehicle with a photo to see the autofill.

The sample cars and plates are fictional, generated with Muse Image. ConVía is an academic, non-commercial project.
